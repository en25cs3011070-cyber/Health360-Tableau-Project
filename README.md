# Smart Healthcare Analytics: Identifying High-Risk Patients

A Tableau project that analyzes patient data to show disease prevalence, lifestyle impact, and high-risk patient groups for heart disease, diabetes and stroke.

## Tools Used
Tableau, CSV dataset, GitHub

## Dataset
`smart_healthcare_dataset.csv` — [paste source link here]
(Not included in this repo. Please download it from the source.)

## Visualizations
1. **KPI Cards**: total patients, disease prevalence and high-risk patients
2. **High-Risk Patient Scatter Plot**: blood pressure vs glucose by risk tier
3. **Comorbidity Analysis**: patients with 0, 1, 2 or 3 diseases
4. **Lifestyle Heatmap**: smoking and alcohol vs disease prevalence
5. **Exercise Level Comparison**: disease prevalence by exercise level
6. **Age Trend Line**: disease prevalence by age
7. **Age Group Analysis**: disease prevalence across age groups
8. **BMI Category Analysis**: disease prevalence across BMI categories
9. **Symptom Analysis**: disease rates for fatigue, chest pain and dizziness

## Dashboard and Story
The dashboards are named after the three users (Dr. Michael Carter, Emily Rodriguez and Dr. Priya Nair), and each combines three charts on one screen. The story presents the dashboards with a short caption.
